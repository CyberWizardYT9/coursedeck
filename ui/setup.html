Welcome to Coursedeck

All your classes, homework and clubs in one place. Let's connect it to your school — this takes about 30 seconds.

What is your school's Canvas address?

Open Canvas in another tab and look at the top of your browser. Copy the bit before the first slash — for example sta.instructure.com.

Good to know

1
Coursedeck uses the Canvas login you already have open in this browser. There is no new password, no code to paste, and no account to make.
2
Everything stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
3
If you ever get signed out of Canvas, just sign back in as normal. Coursedeck picks up again on its own.
