Welcome to Coursedeck

Less digging through Canvas. More room for your day. Connect your school to bring everything together.

What is your school's Canvas address?

Copy the address from your school's Canvas page. You can paste the whole link — we'll find the domain.

Good to know

1
Coursedeck uses the Canvas login you already have open in this browser. There is no new password, no code to paste, and no account to make.
2
Your coursework is saved on this computer. Reminders you create sync to your own Canvas planner when possible. There is no Coursedeck server.
3
If you ever get signed out of Canvas, just sign back in as normal. Coursedeck picks up again on its own.
